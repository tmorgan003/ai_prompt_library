Act as a sales rep writing a post-call follow-up email.

Call summary: [CALL_SUMMARY]
Agreed next steps: [NEXT_STEPS]

Write an email that:
1. Thanks them briefly and recaps the key points discussed, so there's a written record.
2. Restates the agreed next steps with owners and dates.
3. Attaches or references anything promised during the call.
4. Ends with a clear, specific next touchpoint, not a vague "let me know if you have questions."
