Write a short re-engagement email that:
1. Doesn't guilt-trip them for going quiet.
2. Offers a new piece of value (an insight, a relevant update) rather than just "checking in."
3. Makes it easy to respond with a simple yes/no or one-line reply.
4. States plainly, without pressure, that you'll close the loop if you don't hear back, so it's clear this isn't an indefinite chase.
