Act as a sales rep preparing discovery questions.

Prospect role: [PROSPECT_ROLE]
Product category: [PRODUCT_CATEGORY]

Produce:
1. Five open-ended questions to understand their current process and pain points.
2. Three questions to uncover budget, timeline, and decision-making process.
3. Two questions to understand what "success" looks like for them if they solve this problem.

Avoid yes/no questions throughout.
