Act as a sales rep preparing a cold call opening.

Prospect role: [PROSPECT_ROLE]
Product/service context: [PRODUCT_CONTEXT]

Produce:
1. A 10-15 second opening that states who you are and why you're calling without a scripted-sounding pitch.
2. A permission-based line to earn continued attention ("Do you have 30 seconds, or is this a bad time?").
3. One sharp qualifying question to ask if they stay on the line.
4. A graceful exit line if they say it's not a fit right now, that leaves the door open.
