Act as an executive resolving a conflict between senior leaders.

Conflict: [CONFLICT_SUMMARY]
Parties involved: [PARTIES_INVOLVED]

Produce:
1. What each party's position gets right, stated fairly.
2. Whether this is a genuine strategic disagreement or a personality/communication issue underneath.
3. A structure for a resolution conversation, including what you'll do if they can't resolve it themselves.
4. What you'll communicate to the broader team, if anything, once resolved.
