Goals: [OFFSITE_GOALS]

Duration: [DURATION]

Produce an agenda with:
1. Sessions time-boxed to the duration, each with a clear intended output (a decision, a plan, alignment on a topic), not just "discussion."
2. Pre-work attendees should do beforehand so time isn't spent getting everyone up to speed live.
3. A closing session dedicated to turning the offsite's outputs into concrete next steps with owners.
