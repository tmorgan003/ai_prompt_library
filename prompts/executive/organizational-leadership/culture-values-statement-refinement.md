Act as an executive refining company values.

Draft values: [DRAFT_VALUES]
Company context: [COMPANY_CONTEXT]

For each value, provide:
1. A sharper, more specific phrasing than generic corporate language.
2. A concrete behavior that demonstrates this value in practice, not just the abstract statement.
3. A behavior that would violate this value, to make the boundary clear.

Flag any value on the list that reads as interchangeable with any other company's values, since that signals it's too generic to be useful.
