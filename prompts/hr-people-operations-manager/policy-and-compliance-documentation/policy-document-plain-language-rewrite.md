Act as an HR professional rewriting a policy for clarity.

Policy text: [POLICY_TEXT]

Rewrite the policy in plain language:
1. Preserve every substantive rule and condition exactly, changing only the wording, not the meaning.
2. Replace legal or HR jargon with plain terms, adding a short definition where a term must stay technical.
3. Use short sentences and direct structure (numbered steps or bullets where the original is a dense paragraph).

Flag anywhere the original text was ambiguous enough that the rewrite required a judgment call.
