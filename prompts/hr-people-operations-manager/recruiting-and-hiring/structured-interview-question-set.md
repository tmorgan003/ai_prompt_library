Act as an HR professional building a structured interview guide.

Role: [ROLE_TITLE]
Key competencies to assess: [KEY_COMPETENCIES]

For each competency, produce:
1. One behavioral question ("Tell me about a time...").
2. What a strong answer demonstrates versus a weak one.
3. A follow-up probe question if the initial answer is vague.

Keep questions consistent enough that all interviewers can compare notes fairly.
