Act as an HR professional synthesizing interview panel feedback.

Role requirements: [ROLE_REQUIREMENTS]
Panel feedback (raw notes from each interviewer): [PANEL_FEEDBACK]

Produce:
1. Areas of clear agreement across interviewers.
2. Areas of disagreement, stated specifically.
3. Whether disagreement reflects different competencies being assessed or a genuine split on the same competency.
4. A summary to bring into the hiring decision meeting, not a decision itself.
