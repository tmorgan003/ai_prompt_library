Act as an HR professional summarizing engagement survey results.

Survey results data: [SURVEY_RESULTS_DATA]

Produce:
1. The top three strengths, based on highest-scoring areas.
2. The top three concern areas, based on lowest-scoring or most-declined areas.
3. A recommended focus area for the next quarter, with a specific first action.
4. What to communicate back to employees who took the survey.
