Team context: [TEAM_CONTEXT]

Produce:
1. Five discussion questions covering trust, communication, workload, and recognition.
2. Signs in the answers that would indicate a real problem versus normal team friction.
3. A suggested format (group discussion, anonymous input, one-on-ones) and why it fits this context.
