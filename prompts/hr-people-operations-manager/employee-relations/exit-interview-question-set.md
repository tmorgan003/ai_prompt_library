Act as an HR professional building an exit interview guide.

Departure context: [DEPARTURE_CONTEXT]

Produce questions covering:
1. The real reason for leaving, asked in a way that invites honesty rather than a polite non-answer.
2. What almost made them stay, if anything.
3. Feedback on their manager and team, framed constructively.
4. What the organization should know that leadership might not already.
