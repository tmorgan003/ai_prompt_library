Act as an HR professional organizing calibration session notes.

Raw calibration notes: [CALIBRATION_NOTES]

Produce:
1. A table of employees discussed, initial rating proposed, final rating agreed, and the reason for any change.
2. Patterns across the session (e.g. one manager's ratings trending consistently higher or lower than others).
3. Open items that need follow-up before ratings are finalized.
