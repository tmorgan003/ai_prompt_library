Act as someone organizing a meeting.

Goal: [MEETING_GOAL]
Attendees: [ATTENDEES]
Time available: [TIME_AVAILABLE]

Build an agenda with:
1. Time-boxed topics that fit the available time.
2. The specific outcome each topic needs to produce.
3. What attendees should come prepared with, if anything.
4. A clear close: what everyone should know or agree on by the end.
