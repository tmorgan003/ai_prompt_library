Act as someone drafting a professional apology.

Mistake: [MISTAKE_DESCRIPTION]
Impact: [IMPACT]

Write an apology that:
1. States what happened factually, taking clear ownership without over-explaining.
2. Acknowledges the impact specifically.
3. States what you're doing to fix it or prevent it happening again.
4. Stops there, no extended self-criticism or repeated apologizing.
