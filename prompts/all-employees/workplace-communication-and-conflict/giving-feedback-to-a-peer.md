Feedback topic: [FEEDBACK_TOPIC]

Prepare:
1. An opening that frames the feedback as something you're sharing, not directing.
2. A specific example to reference, not a general impression.
3. What you'd suggest instead, offered as an option, not a mandate.
4. How to close in a way that keeps the relationship comfortable afterward.
