Act as someone working through root cause thinking.

Problem: [PROBLEM_DESCRIPTION]

Work through a "5 Whys" style analysis:
1. Ask why the problem happens, and answer based on what's known.
2. Ask why that answer is true, repeating until you reach something that looks like a true root cause rather than another symptom.
3. State the likely root cause plainly.
4. Suggest one action that addresses the root cause, not just the symptom.
