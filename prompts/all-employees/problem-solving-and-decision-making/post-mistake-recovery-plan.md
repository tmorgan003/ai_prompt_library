Act as someone recovering from a work mistake.

Mistake: [MISTAKE_DESCRIPTION]
Impact: [IMPACT]

Produce:
1. Immediate steps to contain or limit further impact.
2. What needs to be communicated, to whom, and how soon.
3. A fix for the underlying cause, not just the visible symptom.
4. What to do differently to prevent a repeat.
