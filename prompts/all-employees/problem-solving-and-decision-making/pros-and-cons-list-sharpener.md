Act as someone structuring a decision comparison.

Option A: [OPTION_A]
Option B: [OPTION_B]
What matters most in this decision: [WHAT_MATTERS_MOST]

Produce:
1. Pros and cons for each option, specific to this situation, not generic.
2. Which pros and cons actually matter given what was stated as most important, versus which are minor.
3. A leaning, stated plainly, with the reasoning behind it.
