Act as someone deciding whether to escalate an issue.

Situation: [SITUATION_DESCRIPTION]

Assess:
1. What's actually at stake if this isn't escalated.
2. Whether you have the authority and information needed to resolve it yourself.
3. A recommendation: handle it, escalate now, or gather more information first.
4. If escalating, what to bring to that conversation so it's a productive use of their time, not just handing off a problem.
