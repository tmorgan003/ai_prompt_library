Write a summary that:
1. Opens with a clear statement of what you do and for whom, not a generic opener.
2. Highlights two or three specific strengths or accomplishments.
3. Ends with what you're focused on or open to now.

Write in first person, professional but not stiff. Keep under 150 words.
