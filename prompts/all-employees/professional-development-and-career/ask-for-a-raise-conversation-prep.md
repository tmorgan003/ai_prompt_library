Prepare:
1. An opening statement that makes the ask directly, not buried in small talk.
2. The two or three strongest accomplishments to reference, tied to business impact.
3. A response to the most likely pushback ("not in the budget," "let's revisit next cycle").
4. A fallback ask if the primary one isn't possible right now.
