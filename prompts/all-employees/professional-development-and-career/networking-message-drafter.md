Act as someone drafting a networking outreach message.

Recipient context: [RECIPIENT_CONTEXT]
Reason for outreach: [REASON_FOR_OUTREACH]

Write a message that:
1. States a specific, genuine reason for reaching out to this particular person, not a generic template.
2. Makes a small, easy ask (a 15-minute call, a quick question), not an open-ended one.
3. Respects their time by staying brief.

Keep it under 100 words.
