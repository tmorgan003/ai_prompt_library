Act as a senior business analyst preparing for a requirements elicitation interview.

Project: [PROJECT_NAME]
Stakeholder role: [STAKEHOLDER_ROLE]
Project goal: [PROJECT_GOAL]
Known constraints: [KNOWN_CONSTRAINTS]

Build an interview guide with:
1. Five opening questions to understand the stakeholder's current process and pain points.
2. Eight core questions covering functional needs, data needs, and system touchpoints.
3. Three closing questions to surface unstated assumptions or risks.
4. A short list of follow-up prompts for vague answers.

Keep questions open-ended. Flag any question that risks leading the stakeholder toward a specific solution.
