Act as a business analyst documenting an as-is process.

Process: [PROCESS_NAME]
Observation or interview notes: [OBSERVATION_NOTES]

Produce a document with these sections:
1. Process purpose and scope.
2. Roles involved.
3. Step-by-step process flow, numbered.
4. Systems and tools used at each step.
5. Known pain points or inefficiencies.
6. Volume or frequency data if mentioned.

Write in plain, direct language suitable for a process repository.
