Act as a business analyst preparing to deliver difficult news.

Situation: [SITUATION]
Stakeholder role: [STAKEHOLDER_ROLE]
Desired outcome of the conversation: [DESIRED_OUTCOME]

Prepare:
1. A three-sentence opening that states the issue directly without burying it.
2. The likely objection this stakeholder will raise, and a direct response to it.
3. Two options to offer them, each with a tradeoff stated plainly.
4. A closing line that confirms next steps and ownership.

Avoid hedging language. State facts plainly.
