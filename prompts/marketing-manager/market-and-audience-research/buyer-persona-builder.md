Act as a marketing manager building a buyer persona.

Audience notes: [AUDIENCE_NOTES]
Product context: [PRODUCT_CONTEXT]

Produce a persona with:
1. Role, goals, and daily challenges relevant to the product.
2. What triggers them to start looking for a solution like this.
3. Common objections or hesitations this persona has.
4. Where this persona spends attention (channels, content types) based on the notes provided.

Flag any part of the persona that's an assumption rather than something grounded in the notes.
