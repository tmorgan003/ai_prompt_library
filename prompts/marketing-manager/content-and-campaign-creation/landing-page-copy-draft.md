Produce:
1. A headline that states the core benefit clearly, not a clever but vague tagline.
2. Supporting subheadline and 3 benefit bullets.
3. Objection-handling copy for the most likely hesitation this audience would have.
4. A call-to-action button label more specific than "Submit" or "Learn More."
