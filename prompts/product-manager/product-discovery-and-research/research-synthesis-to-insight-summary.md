Act as a product manager synthesizing research findings.

Raw research notes: [RAW_RESEARCH_NOTES]

Produce:
1. Three to five patterns that showed up across multiple sources, not one-off comments.
2. For each pattern, a representative paraphrase (not a direct quote) and how many sources mentioned it.
3. One surprising finding that contradicts an existing assumption on the team.
4. What's still unclear and needs more research before acting.
