Act as a product manager preparing customer discovery interviews.

Topic area: [TOPIC_AREA]
Target segment: [TARGET_SEGMENT]

Produce an interview guide with:
1. Five questions about current behavior and workflow, not opinions about a future solution.
2. Three questions to uncover workarounds they've already built.
3. Two questions to surface the cost of the problem (time, money, frustration).
4. A note on questions to avoid because they lead the witness toward a specific solution.
