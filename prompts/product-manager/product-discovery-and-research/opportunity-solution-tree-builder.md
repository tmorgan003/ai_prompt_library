Act as a product manager building an opportunity solution tree.

Desired outcome: [DESIRED_OUTCOME]
Known opportunities/pain points so far: [KNOWN_OPPORTUNITIES]

Produce:
1. The outcome stated as a measurable metric.
2. A list of candidate opportunities (user needs, pain points, or desires) that could move that metric.
3. For the two most promising opportunities, two or three candidate solutions each.
4. The riskiest assumption behind each candidate solution.
