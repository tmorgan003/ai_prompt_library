Feature: [FEATURE_NAME]
Key benefits: [KEY_BENEFITS]
Common questions expected: [COMMON_QUESTIONS]

Produce:
1. A two-sentence description usable in a customer conversation.
2. Three key benefits, each with a plain-language explanation of why it matters to the customer.
3. Answers to the common questions, written the way a rep would actually say them.
4. What NOT to promise yet (known limitations).
