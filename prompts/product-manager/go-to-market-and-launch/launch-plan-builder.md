Produce a plan covering:
1. Internal readiness checklist (support team training, documentation, sales enablement if relevant).
2. External communication plan (channels, timing, key messages).
3. Success metrics and how they'll be tracked post-launch.
4. A rollback or messaging plan if the launch has to be paused.
