Act as a product manager writing the metrics narrative for a QBR.

Quarter: [QUARTER]
Metrics data: [METRICS_DATA]

Produce:
1. A one-paragraph summary of overall performance against goals.
2. The two or three metrics that matter most this quarter and why they moved the way they did.
3. One metric that underperformed, with an honest explanation, not a spin.
4. What this means for next quarter's priorities.
