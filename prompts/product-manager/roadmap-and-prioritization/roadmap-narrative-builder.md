Strategic theme for this period: [STRATEGIC_THEME]
Initiative list: [INITIATIVE_LIST]

Produce:
1. A one-paragraph narrative connecting the initiatives to the strategic theme.
2. For each initiative, a one-sentence "why this, why now."
3. What's explicitly not on the roadmap this period, and why, to manage expectations.
