Options considered: [OPTIONS_CONSIDERED]
Decision made: [DECISION_MADE]

Produce a brief with:
1. What was chosen and what was deprioritized.
2. The reasoning, stated in terms of impact and cost, not just preference.
3. Who this decision affects and what they need to know.
4. The condition under which this decision would be revisited.
