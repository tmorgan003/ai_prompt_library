Goal: [MEETING_GOAL]
Attendees: [ATTENDEES]
Time available: [TIME_AVAILABLE]

Produce an agenda with:
1. Time-boxed segments that add up to the available time, each with a clear purpose.
2. The specific decision or artifact each segment must produce.
3. A parking lot process for off-topic items.
4. What "done" looks like for the whole session.
