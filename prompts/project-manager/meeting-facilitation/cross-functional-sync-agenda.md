Teams involved: [TEAMS_INVOLVED]
Shared goal: [SHARED_GOAL]

Produce a recurring agenda template:
1. A quick round-robin status check, capped at one minute per team.
2. A focused discussion section for active blockers or cross-team dependencies only.
3. A parking lot for team-specific issues that don't need the full group.
4. A closing recap of action items and owners.
