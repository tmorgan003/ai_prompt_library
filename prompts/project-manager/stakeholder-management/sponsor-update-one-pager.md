Act as a project manager writing a sponsor update.

Project: [PROJECT_NAME]
Detailed status: [DETAILED_STATUS]

Write a one-page update with:
1. Status in one sentence.
2. Progress against the last commitment made to this sponsor.
3. The single most important decision or input needed from them right now.
4. What's coming next.

Assume the sponsor has 90 seconds to read this.
