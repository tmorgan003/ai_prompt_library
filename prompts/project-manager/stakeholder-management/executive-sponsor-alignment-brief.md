Act as a project manager preparing a sponsor alignment brief.

Project: [PROJECT_NAME]
Decision needed: [DECISION_NEEDED]
Options: [OPTIONS]

Produce a brief with:
1. The decision needed, stated as a single clear question.
2. Each option with cost, timeline, and risk implications.
3. A recommendation, clearly marked as the project team's view, not a final answer.
4. What happens if no decision is made by the needed date.
