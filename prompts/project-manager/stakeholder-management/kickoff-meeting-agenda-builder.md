Act as a project manager building a kickoff meeting agenda.

Project: [PROJECT_NAME]
Attendees: [ATTENDEES]
Goals: [PROJECT_GOALS]

Produce an agenda with:
1. Time-boxed sections (introductions, project overview, roles and responsibilities, timeline, how we'll communicate, Q&A).
2. For each section, the one outcome it needs to produce before moving on.
3. A closing action: what everyone leaves the meeting knowing or committed to.
