Act as a project manager escalating a vendor issue.

Vendor: [VENDOR_NAME]
Missed commitments: [MISSED_COMMITMENTS]
Desired outcome: [DESIRED_OUTCOME]

Write a formal escalation that:
1. States the specific commitments missed, with dates.
2. References the relevant contract or SLA terms if applicable.
3. States the business impact plainly.
4. States exactly what is needed and by when to resolve this.

Keep tone professional and factual, not accusatory.
