Produce a checklist covering:
1. Contract and legal steps (NDA, MSA, SOW execution).
2. Access provisioning needed for the vendor to start work.
3. Communication setup (points of contact, meeting cadence, escalation path).
4. Success criteria and check-in schedule for the first 30 days.
